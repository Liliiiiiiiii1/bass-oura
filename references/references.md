## Methodological Note

The market-potential scenarios of 5 million, 10 million, and 20 million are assumptions used for sensitivity analysis, not externally verified market-size estimates. Fitbit device sales are used as a proxy for wearable adoption and do not measure unique first-time adopters of Oura Ring 4.
